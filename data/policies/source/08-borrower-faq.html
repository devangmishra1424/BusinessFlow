Borrower Questions and Answers

Short answers to the questions borrowers ask most. Each answer names the policy that governs it.

Paying your EMI

My EMI auto-debit bounced. What happens now?

The EMI is still due. Within 3 days of the due date there is no charge: pay it through the payment link our assistant sends, or by UPI or bank transfer with your loan number in the remarks. From the 4th day a flat ₹500 late payment charge applies once to that EMI. We do not charge for the bounce itself; your bank may. (BFF-POL-02, BFF-GUIDE-06)

Can I get a few more days?

If you are within 3 days of the due date, you already have them: the grace period covers you and nothing needs to be approved. Beyond that, you can make a promise to pay, in one payment or in up to 4 parts within 90 days. (BFF-POL-03)

Can I pay half now and half later?

Yes, as a promise to pay in parts, each part an amount by a date. Or, for this month only, you can pay at least 70% of the EMI as a part-payment; the rest of the EMI stays due. (BFF-POL-03, BFF-POL-04)

I paid but it is not showing.

Tell our assistant. It records a payment claim and our team checks the bank record within 2 working days. Add the amount, the date, how you paid and the UTR or UPI reference if you have it. Reminders on that loan pause while the claim is open. (BFF-POL-05)

Which comes first, the EMI or the late fee?

The EMI. A payment goes to the EMI due first, then to any late payment charge still owed, and anything left is credited to your next EMI. Paying the EMI keeps the loan up to date even if a charge is still owed. (BFF-POL-02)

How long is a payment link valid?

72 hours, for one use, for the amount shown on it. Ask for a new one if it has expired. (BFF-GUIDE-06)

I want to pay off my loan early. Is there a charge?

No. There is no prepayment or foreclosure charge. Extra payments are credited to your next EMI unless you choose to reduce the EMI or the tenure. (BFF-POL-07)

Charges

Why was I charged ₹500?

Because an EMI was still unpaid on the 4th day after its due date. The charge is flat, once per EMI, never per day, never compounded and never charged interest. (BFF-POL-02)

Can the late fee be removed?

Only a person on our team can waive it. Ask our assistant to send a waiver request with your reason; until our team decides, the charge is still owed. If you paid within the grace period and the payment was credited late, the charge is reversed when the payment is recorded with its real date. (BFF-POL-02)

I think a charge is wrong.

Raise a dispute. Automated reminders and offers on that loan stop until a person reviews it, and you are told the outcome and the reason. (BFF-POL-05)

Difficulty paying

My business is slow this season. What can I do?

Tell us early. The options are a promise to pay in parts, a part-payment of at least 70% of one EMI, a tenure extension of 1 to 3 months that lowers the EMI with no extra interest, or a one-time settlement of the remaining balance less 5%. Our assistant can work out the figures from your account; a person on our team approves any restructuring. (BFF-POL-04)

Why can the assistant not offer me an extension?

Automated offers are not made on a loan with an open dispute or payment claim, or after 2 broken promises to pay. Your request goes to a person instead, who can still agree an arrangement with you. (BFF-POL-04)

What happens if I miss a promise?

The promise is recorded as broken and routine reminders resume. After a second broken promise, a person on our team takes over your account. (BFF-POL-03)

Requests, replies and complaints

How do I know what happened to my request?

Look under My requests on your dashboard, send /requests in Telegram, or ask the assistant "what happened to my request?". Each request shows whether it is open or closed, the outcome and everything our team said. (BFF-POL-03)

Your team sent me a question. How do I answer?

Reply to that message: in Telegram, reply to it directly; on the dashboard, use the Reply box under the message; or tell the assistant your answer. It reaches the person who asked. (BFF-POL-01)

I want to complain about how I was treated.

Say so in the chat or in Telegram. It is recorded as a grievance and handled by a person, with two further levels of escalation and, after 30 days or an unsatisfactory reply, the RBI Ombudsman. (BFF-POL-05)

Can I talk to a person?

Yes, at any time. Ask the assistant for a callback or for a person (in Telegram, send /agent), and the request goes to our team with your reason. (BFF-POL-01)

Your loans and the assistant

I have two loans with you. How do I see both?

Each loan has its own number and its own EMI, due date and history. In Telegram one chat can hold all your loans: send /loans to see them and switch between them, and every reminder says which loan it is for. In the web chat, sign in to each loan separately. (BFF-POL-01)

How do I link Telegram?

Open the invite link our team sends when your loan is opened, or open our bot and send your loan number and access key. Once linked, you receive reminders and replies there and can use /status, /pay, /history, /promise, /requests and /loans. (BFF-GUIDE-06)

Can I talk to the assistant in Hindi?

Yes. It answers in English, Hindi or Hinglish, by text or by voice. Reminders are sent in the language recorded for your loan; ask our team to change it. In Telegram you can switch the chat's language at any time with /hindi or /english. (BFF-POL-01)

Can the assistant make mistakes?

It answers only from your account and our written policies, and each reply is checked automatically before it is sent. If it cannot answer from those, it says so. Amounts and dates it gives are read from your account at that moment. Anything it cannot decide goes to a person. (BFF-POL-01)

How do I get my no-dues certificate?

Once your loan shows no EMIs left, ask the assistant for your closure certificate. The request goes to our team, who check the account and issue it. For a vehicle loan you also receive an NOC and Form 35. (BFF-POL-07)
