Disputes, Payment Claims and Grievance Redressal Policy

In short. There are three different things you can raise, and each is handled differently. A payment claim ("I have already paid and it is not showing") is checked against our bank record within 2 working days. A dispute ("this amount, charge or date is wrong") is decided by a person on our team. A grievance ("I am unhappy with how I was treated") is a complaint handled under the levels below, with the right to approach the RBI Ombudsman if you are not satisfied. While a payment claim or a dispute is open, automated reminders and automated offers on that loan stop.

1. Payment claims

A payment claim is your statement that you have already paid an amount – an EMI, a late payment charge or any other amount – that does not yet appear on your loan. It is not a disagreement about what you owe; it is a request to find your money.

1.1 How a claim is checked

  1. Tell our assistant or our team that you have paid. The claim is recorded in your own words with a reference (for example D-118), and reminders on that loan pause.
  2. Add whatever helps us find the payment: the amount, the date, how you paid (UPI, NEFT, IMPS, cash deposit, payment link) and the transaction reference (UTR or UPI reference number). You can add details to an open claim at any time, in the chat or under My requests.
  3. Our team checks the bank statement for the payment within 2 working days. When our bank statement carries your loan number, your phone number or the reference you gave on the claim, the payment is matched automatically and posted the moment the statement is uploaded.
  4. When the payment is found, it is recorded on your loan with the date the bank received it, so a payment made within the grace period stays on time and any late payment charge assessed in the meantime is reversed. The claim is closed and you are told in the claim's thread.
  5. When the payment cannot be found, our team tells you what was checked and what is needed, for example a bank statement showing the debit. The claim stays open while you reply.

Only one claim or dispute can be open on a loan at a time. New details you send are added to the one that is open, so nothing is split across two requests.

2. Disputes

A dispute is your claim that the amount we say you owe, a charge, or a due date on your loan is wrong – for example a late payment charge applied to an EMI you paid within the grace period, or an EMI amount that does not match your agreement.

  • Opening a dispute stops all automated collection action on that loan straight away: no automated reminders, no automated restructuring quote, no automated part-payment agreement. The loan waits for a person.
  • Your reason is recorded in your own words, with any amount or date you gave. It is not summarised in a way that loses what you are claiming.
  • Our assistant does not investigate or decide a dispute and never tells you that a disputed amount will be waived, reduced or corrected. A person on our team decides, records the outcome and the reason, and tells you.
  • If the dispute is upheld, the correction (for example a waived charge) is made and shown on your account. If it is not upheld, you are told why, and reminders resume.

2.1 Unauthorised transactions and fraud

If you believe a loan, a payment or a debit was made without your authority, say so plainly. It is recorded as a suspected fraud, separately from an ordinary dispute, and goes straight to a person. Do not share your OTP, PIN or password with anyone, including anyone claiming to be from BusinessFlow Finance: we never ask for them.

3. Grievance redressal

A grievance is a complaint about how your loan or your case was handled – by our assistant or by a person on our team – even when no amount or date is in question. "I want to file a complaint" and "I am not happy with how this was handled" are grievances. Fair, transparent and respectful treatment is your right under the Fair Practices Code (policy BFF-POL-01), and acknowledging a complaint is standard practice, not a favour.

3.1 The RBI Ombudsman

If you have not received a reply within 30 days of lodging your complaint with us, or you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Reserve Bank of India under the Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021. A complaint can be lodged online on the RBI's Complaint Management System portal (cms.rbi.org.in). It must normally be lodged within one year of our reply, or, if we did not reply, within one year and 30 days of the date you complained to us, and the matter must not already be pending before a court or another forum. Our team gives you the current details of the scheme on request; our assistant does not quote phone numbers or addresses from memory.

4. Records

Every payment claim, dispute and grievance keeps its full thread – your words, every message from our team, and the outcome with the reason – on your account. You can read it at any time under My requests, or ask our assistant "what happened to my request?".
