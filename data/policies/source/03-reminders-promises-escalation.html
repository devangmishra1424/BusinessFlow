Reminders, Promise to Pay and Escalation Policy

In short. We remind you before an EMI is due, on the due date and during the 3-day grace period, and every day after that until it is paid. When you cannot pay on time you can promise to pay: in one payment or in up to 4 parts, each a fixed amount by a fixed date, all within 90 days. A promise is judged by its exact words: each part must be paid in full by its date. While a promise is open we remind you only of your promise. Your account is passed to a person on our team when you have broken 2 promises, when an EMI is 14 or more days overdue, when you raise a dispute or a complaint, or whenever you ask for something our assistant cannot approve.

1. Reminder schedule

Reminders are worked out once a day from your account and sent through the channel you have linked (Telegram) and shown in our web assistant. Each kind of reminder is sent at most once a day, and each one says which loan it is about and the exact amount due, including any late payment charge already owed, with a payment link for that amount.

1.1 When reminders stop

  • An open dispute or payment claim. While a person on our team is checking something you raised, no automated reminder is sent and no automated offer is made on that loan.
  • An open promise to pay. The routine reminders are replaced by reminders of your own promise. If the promise is broken, the routine reminders resume the next day.
  • A loan fully repaid. A loan with no EMIs left is never reminded.

2. Promise to pay

2.1 What a promise is

A promise to pay is your own commitment to pay a stated amount by a stated date. You can make one with our assistant in the chat or in Telegram, or on a call with our team. It must be specific: one payment, or up to 4 parts, each part an amount by a date, every date between today and 90 days from today, each date later than the one before, and the total no more than what the loan could owe.

2.2 How a promise is recorded

  1. You tell us how much you will pay and by when, in one go or in parts.
  2. The assistant writes the terms out and reads them back to you, in your language.
  3. Only after you clearly confirm them are they recorded. Nothing is recorded on an unclear answer.

A new promise replaces one that is still open; the open parts of the old one are marked as replaced, not broken.

2.3 How a promise is judged

A promise is judged by its exact words, and only by them. There is no extra window after a date.

  • Part 1 is kept once the money you have paid since making the promise reaches part 1's amount on or before part 1's date. Part 2 is kept once that money reaches the total of parts 1 and 2 on or before part 2's date, and so on.
  • Paying a part in pieces is fine. Paying it late is not: a part whose date has passed without the money is broken.
  • When one part is broken, the whole promise is broken and its later parts are void. A broken promise counts once, however many parts it had.
  • When every part is kept, the promise is kept, and it is recorded as kept on your account.

3. Escalation to a person

Our assistant handles routine questions and requests. Your account is passed to a person on our team, with the reason and the history written down so that you do not have to explain again, in each of these cases:

TriggerWhy
A second broken promise to payOne broken promise happens; a pattern needs a conversation with a person. From the second broken promise no automated restructuring offer is made on the loan.
An EMI 14 or more days past dueDaily reminders alone are not working; a person reviews the account while the reminders continue.
An open disputeOnly a person can decide whether an amount, a charge or a date is wrong.
A complaint about how you were treatedHandled as a grievance (policy BFF-POL-05), recorded as such so it is routed correctly.
A request the assistant cannot approveA fee waiver, a tenure extension, a settlement, a closure certificate, a callback, anything outside policy.
The assistant is unsureWhen a request does not clearly fit a policy, it is passed on rather than guessed.

Escalation is not a penalty and is never reported as one. It means a person now owns the next step. Each escalation has a reference (for example ESC-0042), and you can see it, every reply from our team and its outcome under My requests, or by asking the assistant "what happened to my request?". You can also answer a message from our team directly; your reply joins the same request.

4. Calls from our team

When a person on our team calls you, the outcome of the call (reached, no answer, voicemail, wrong number) and any note are recorded on your account, so the next person who looks at it knows what was said. A promise made on a call is recorded with the same rules as one made in the chat and is read back to you before the call ends.
