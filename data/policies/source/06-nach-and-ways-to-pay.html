NACH Mandates and Ways to Pay

In short. Your EMI is normally collected by a NACH auto-debit mandate on the due date. If a debit fails, the EMI is still due: pay it through the payment link our assistant sends (valid for 72 hours), by UPI or by bank transfer quoting your loan number. We do not charge for a failed debit, but your bank may. A lapsed or cancelled mandate stops every future debit until a new one is registered with your bank; our assistant cannot register one for you.

1. What a NACH mandate is

NACH (National Automated Clearing House) is the system run by the National Payments Corporation of India that lets a lender collect a fixed amount from your bank account on fixed dates, with your one-time authorisation. The authorisation is the mandate. Each registered mandate has a Unique Mandate Reference Number (UMRN), a maximum amount, a frequency and an end date. Once it is registered, your EMI is debited on each due date without you doing anything.

A mandate can be registered as an e-mandate, authenticated online through your net banking or debit card (or Aadhaar-based e-sign where your bank supports it), or as a physical mandate, a signed paper form sent to your bank. E-mandates are usually registered faster; a physical mandate depends on your bank checking the signature against its records.

2. Why a debit fails

Reason the bank givesWhat it meansWhat to do
Insufficient balanceThe account did not hold the EMI amount on the debit date.Pay the EMI now through the payment link or by UPI; keep the EMI amount in the account a day before each due date.
Mandate not registered, cancelled or expiredThere is no live authorisation; every debit will fail until a new mandate is registered.Pay this EMI another way, and register a new mandate with your bank.
Account closed, frozen or dormantThe bank will not debit the account at all.Pay another way and register a mandate on an active account.
Amount exceeds the mandate limitThe EMI is higher than the maximum you authorised (for example after a restructuring).Register a mandate with a higher limit.
Signature mismatch (physical mandate)The bank could not match the signature on the form.Re-sign, or register an e-mandate instead.
Technical or bank-side failureThe debit was rejected or timed out at a bank, unrelated to your balance or mandate.Pay another way; if the bank's error caused a late payment charge, ask for a waiver (policy BFF-POL-02).

Your account record shows only whether your mandate is currently active. It does not show why a particular past debit failed; that reason is in your bank's return advice. Our assistant will tell you whether the mandate is active and will not guess the reason for a specific failed debit. If you need the reason, ask for the request to go to our team.

BusinessFlow Finance does not levy a charge for a failed or returned debit. Your bank may charge a return fee under its own tariff. A late payment charge applies only if the EMI is still unpaid after the grace period (policy BFF-POL-02).

3.1 Paying more or less than the amount due

On the payment page you can pay more than the EMI; the page asks how the extra should be used (credit the next EMI, reduce the EMI, or reduce the tenure). A payment link for less than the EMI is a part-payment: it counts towards this EMI, and the page shows how much of the EMI will still be due afterwards. See policy BFF-POL-02 for how every payment is applied.

4. Registering a new mandate

  1. Ask our team for a new mandate request. You will receive an e-mandate link or a physical form for the loan's EMI, frequency and end date.
  2. Authenticate the e-mandate with your net banking or debit card, or sign the physical form exactly as in your bank's records and return it.
  3. Your bank registers it, usually within a few working days. Until our records show the mandate as active, pay each EMI by payment link, UPI or transfer so that it is not late.

Our assistant has no way to register, reactivate or cancel a mandate. It can tell you whether your mandate is active, send you a payment link for the EMI due, record a promise to pay, and pass a mandate request to our team.
