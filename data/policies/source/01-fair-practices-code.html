Fair Practices Code

In short. We tell you every term and charge before you sign, in a language you understand. We contact you only about your own loan, never with threats or abusive language, and never by contacting your family, neighbours or customers about your dues. Our automated assistant can explain your account, send payment links, record promises and pass requests to our team, but it cannot waive a charge, change your interest rate or approve a restructuring on its own: a person on our team decides those. If you are unhappy with how you were treated, you can complain and escalate as set out in policy BFF-POL-05.

1. Why this code exists

BusinessFlow Finance lends to small businesses: shops, workshops, transporters, manufacturers and service providers. This code sets out the standards of fair, transparent and respectful treatment that every borrower is entitled to, from the loan application to the day the loan is closed. It follows the Fair Practices Code that the Reserve Bank of India requires every non-banking financial company to adopt, and it binds every employee, every system we use to service loans (including our automated assistant), and every agency acting for us.

2. Applying for a loan

  • Application forms list every document we need, so that you can submit them in one go. We acknowledge every application and tell you the time within which we will decide.
  • If we decline your application, we tell you the main reasons in writing.
  • Before you sign, we give you a Key Fact Statement (KFS) that sets out, in a standard format, the loan amount, the annual interest rate, the Annual Percentage Rate (APR) including all fees, the EMI, the number of EMIs, every charge that can be levied (including the late payment charge) and the cooling-off period.
  • The loan agreement, the KFS and the sanction letter are provided in English and, on request, in Hindi or the language you understand, and a copy of the signed agreement is given to you.

3. Terms and conditions during the loan

  • We give at least 30 days' notice of any change in a charge, and no change applies to an EMI already due.
  • The interest rate on your loan is fixed for its whole tenure and is stated in your loan agreement. It is not changed during the loan.
  • Penal charges for late payment are flat charges, not penal interest; they are never capitalised and no interest is computed on them (see policy BFF-POL-02).
  • We do not charge any fee for a prepayment or for closing the loan early.
  • We do not interfere in the running of your business beyond what the loan agreement provides for.

4. How we communicate with you about repayment

4.1 Channels and language

We contact you through the channels you have agreed to: our web assistant, Telegram if you have linked it, or a phone call from our team. Messages are sent in the language you chose when your loan was opened (English, Hindi or Hinglish); you can ask our team to change it at any time. Every message names the loan it is about. If you have more than one loan with us, each reminder says which loan it is for, and in Telegram you can switch between your loans with the /loans command.

4.2 Frequency

  • Automated reminders are sent at most once a day for each kind of reminder (policy BFF-POL-03 sets out the full schedule).
  • They stop while a dispute or a payment claim you raised is open, and while you have a promise to pay on record you are reminded only of your own promise.
  • A message from our team that asks you a question can be answered directly: reply to it in Telegram or in the chat, and your answer reaches the person who asked.

4.3 Conduct we will never engage in

  • Threats, intimidation, abusive or insulting language, or any form of harassment, in person, by phone or in writing.
  • Contacting your relatives, friends, neighbours, employees, customers or suppliers about your dues, or disclosing your loan to anyone not entitled to know about it.
  • Sending messages that are misleading about the consequences of non-payment, or that pretend to be from a court, the police or a government authority.
  • Repeated calls intended to pressure rather than to inform, or calls to a number you have told us is wrong.
  • Taking possession of any asset other than through the process in your loan agreement and the law.

BusinessFlow Finance does not use third-party recovery agents for the loans covered by this code. All follow-up is carried out by our own collections team and our automated assistant, which are bound by this code.

5. Our automated assistant

We use an AI assistant to answer your questions about your loan at any hour and to carry out routine requests. It is always identified as an automated assistant, and you can ask for a person at any time.

The assistant canThe assistant cannot
Tell you what you owe now, your due date, your payments and your charges, read from your account. Waive, reduce or reverse any charge, or promise that one will be waived.
Send a payment link for the amount due (valid for 72 hours) and record a promise to pay after reading it back to you. Change your interest rate, EMI or tenure, or approve a restructuring or a settlement.
Quote, from your account, what a tenure extension or a settlement would look like, and send it to our team for approval. Move money, take a payment itself, or register or cancel a NACH mandate.
Record a payment you say you have already made, a dispute, a complaint, a request for a fee waiver or a closure certificate, and tell you what happened to each. Decide a dispute, a complaint or a payment claim. A person on our team decides, and you are told the outcome.

Every statement the assistant makes about an amount or a date comes from your account record or from our written policies, and every reply is checked automatically before it is sent. If it cannot answer from those sources, it says so and offers to pass the question to our team.

6. Your information

We use your personal and business information only to provide and service your loan, to meet our legal obligations and to prevent fraud. We report your repayment record to the credit information companies as the law requires. We do not sell your information or share it for marketing. Conversations with our assistant are stored with your account so that our team can see what you asked and what you were told; you can ask our team for a copy.

7. Hardship

If your business is going through a difficult period, tell us early. We consider every request for more time on its merits: a promise to pay in up to 4 parts within 90 days (policy BFF-POL-03), a one-cycle part-payment, a tenure extension of up to 3 months or a one-time settlement (policy BFF-POL-04). Asking for help never counts against you.

8. Complaints

You can complain about anything in this code, including how our assistant or our team treated you, through the chat, Telegram or a call. A complaint about how you were treated is handled as a grievance, separately from questions about amounts or dates. Policy BFF-POL-05 sets out the levels of escalation, the timelines and your right to approach the RBI Ombudsman.

9. Review

The Board reviews compliance with this code and the working of the grievance redressal mechanism every year. This code is published on our website and in the help section of our assistant.
