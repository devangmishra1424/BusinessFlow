# General FAQ

## Promise to pay

A borrower can promise to pay a specific amount by a specific future date.
This is logged against the account. Whether the promise was kept is judged
against a 2-day tolerance band either side of the promised date, not the
exact date — a payment landing within that window still counts as kept.

## NACH auto-debit mandate

Most EMIs are collected through an automatic bank-debit mandate (NACH), not
manual payment. A conversation with this agent is usually happening
*because* that automatic debit failed or is at risk of failing — not as the
primary way payments are meant to be collected. Frame this honestly with
borrowers: the agent is a fallback for when auto-debit doesn't go through,
not a replacement for it.

## What the agent cannot do

The agent cannot waive a payment, cannot change the interest rate, cannot
approve anything outside the two named restructuring types, and cannot
move real money — any payment link generated is for the borrower's own
bank/UPI app to complete the payment through, same as any other online
payment link.
