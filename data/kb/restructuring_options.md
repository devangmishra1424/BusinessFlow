# Restructuring options

When a borrower cannot pay the standard EMI, two pre-approved restructuring
types are available. Both recalculate the real numbers before anything is
confirmed — never quote a restructured amount without running the
calculation against the actual account.

## Extend tenure

The remaining tenure can be extended by up to 3 months. This lowers the
monthly EMI by spreading the same remaining principal over more months.
Extending tenure preserves the account's delinquency history — it is a
genuine restructuring, not a reset.

## One-time settlement

A single lump-sum payment can close the loan early at a 5% discount off the
remaining principal. This is a full and final settlement — once paid, the
loan is closed.

## What blocks an automated offer

Neither option can be offered automatically if the account has an open
dispute, or if the account has 2 or more broken promises-to-pay on record.
Both situations go to a human instead — the automated agent does not
negotiate restructuring on an account in that state.

## Partial payment for a single cycle

Separately from formal restructuring, a borrower can propose paying less
than the full EMI for the current cycle only. The minimum acceptable amount
is 70% of the standard EMI. The same dispute/broken-promise block applies —
this is not available on an account that needs a human.
