# Escalation policy

Escalate an account to a human whenever any of the following is true:

- An open dispute exists on the account.
- The account has 2 or more broken promises-to-pay on record — a single
  broken promise is normal and doesn't trigger escalation on its own, but a
  pattern does.
- The borrower asks for something outside what the agent is authorized to
  approve (anything other than the two pre-approved restructuring types, or
  a partial payment at or above the policy minimum).
- The agent is genuinely unsure whether a request fits within policy.

When escalating, the reason given to the human should be specific and
factual — the actual request or claim the borrower made, not a generic
"borrower needs help." The human should not have to start the conversation
cold; whatever numbers, dates, or prior promises are relevant should already
be on the account record for them to see.

Escalating is not a failure state. An agent that recognizes it should not
handle something and hands it off cleanly, with context, is working
correctly.
