# Grace period policy

Every EMI has a 3-day grace period after the due date. A payment received
within these 3 days is not treated as late for reporting purposes and no
late fee is applied.

Past day 3, the account is considered past due. There is no extra charge
specifically for using the grace period itself — it is a standard part of
every loan, not a special favour granted case by case.

The grace period applies once per EMI cycle. It does not carry over or
stack if a borrower is late on consecutive cycles.

A borrower asking "can I get a few more days" inside the 3-day window
should simply be told the grace period already covers them — there is
nothing further to approve. A request for more time beyond day 3 is a
restructuring or promise-to-pay conversation, not a grace-period one.
