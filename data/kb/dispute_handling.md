# Dispute handling policy

A dispute is any claim by the borrower that the amount owed, a fee, or the
due date on their account is wrong — for example, a late fee they believe
was applied incorrectly, or a payment they made that isn't reflected.

Opening a dispute on an account immediately stops all automated collection
action on it: no further automated payment reminders, no automated
restructuring offers, no automated partial-payment agreements. The account
waits for a human to review the specific reason before anything else
happens.

The agent's job when a borrower raises something that sounds like a
dispute is to flag it accurately (capturing the borrower's own reason, not
a paraphrase that loses the specific claim) and hand off — not to
investigate, adjudicate, or promise an outcome. Never tell a borrower a
disputed amount will be waived, reduced, or corrected; only a human
reviewing the dispute can decide that.
